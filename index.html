Ghar Pe Banaane Ki Quick Recipes 👨‍🍳

Agar bahar nahi jaana toh bhai, kitchen mein jugaad kar lenge!

☕ Adrak wali chai — 2 cups
  • 1 cup water + 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp tea leaves, sugar as you like
  • Crushed ginger; optional cardamom
  • Water, ginger and tea ko 2–3 min boil karo. Milk aur sugar add karke simmer karo, phir strain kar lo.
🥔 Easy vada pav filling
  • 2 boiled potatoes, mashed
  • Garlic, green chilli, mustard seeds, turmeric and salt
  • Oil mein mustard, garlic and chilli sauté karo. Turmeric aur potato add karke mix karo.
  • Potato balls ko besan batter mein dip karke fry karo; pav, chutney and fried green chilli ke saath serve karo.
🥟 Quick aloo samosa filling
  • Boiled potato, peas (optional), cumin, chilli, coriander and salt
  • Masale ko pan mein warm karo, potatoes add karke mix karo.
  • Ready-made samosa patti mein filling bhar ke seal karo and fry/bake according to packet directions.

Hot oil use karte waqt careful rehna, masterchef Anuj. 😄
